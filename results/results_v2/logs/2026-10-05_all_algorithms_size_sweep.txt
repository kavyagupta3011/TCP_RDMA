OBSERVATIONS
- LL vs Sentinel: Sentinel is faster at 128 B (2.10 vs 2.36 us) and at 64 KiB (92.0 vs 95.5 us); LL is faster from
  1 KiB to 16 KiB. This does not match the paper's "LL best at tiny sizes, Sentinel at larger" ordering exactly;
  here LL is flag-after-data over RC ordering (not a 16-byte atomic flag+data store), so the mechanisms differ.
- Twoshot_Sentinel is clearly worse than Twoshot_LL at larger sizes (93.1 vs 73.0 us at 64 KiB, 32.7 vs 22.3 us at
  16 KiB). Likely cause (hypothesis, not measured): the sentinel path checks AND re-arms every float on the CPU,
  while LL waits on one flag word and then reads the data directly. This is the biggest remaining optimization target.
- Cost per exchange: Bidirectional's 8-chunk pass at 128 B is 14.2 us, i.e. ~1.8 us per chunk round trip between 2 ranks.
- Not yet compared against the InfiniBand hardware floor (e.g. ib_write_lat), so we cannot yet say how close
  ~2.1 us is to the limit of this fabric. The paper's 1.404 us is an NVLink figure and not a valid target here.
