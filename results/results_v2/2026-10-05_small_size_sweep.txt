OBSERVATIONS
- Latency is steady: median within ~1% of mean at every size, p99 within ~15-30% of the mean.
- The paper's trend is reproduced: two-shot pays for a second synchronization round, so it is much slower
  at tiny sizes (~1.9x at 128 B) and only pays off once data volume dominates (64 KiB, ~15% faster).
- Best number so far: 4.28 us for a 128 B one-shot Sentinel AllReduce on 3 ranks. The paper's measured
  SoL on GB200 NVLink is 1.404 us, but that is a different interconnect (NVLink, GPU L2) and is NOT a
  valid target for this InfiniBand/CPU cluster. Compare against InfiniBand's own floor instead
  (e.g. ib_write_lat on this fabric), which we have not measured yet.

HYPOTHESES FOR THE NEXT OPTIMIZATION STEP (not yet verified)
- 4.28 us at 128 B is roughly two back-to-back blocking, signaled RDMA writes (rdma_write() posts then
  polls the CQ for each peer in turn). Posting both writes first and polling once could cut this
  substantially.
- Cost grows ~linearly with size beyond 4 KiB (64 KiB sent to 2 peers in 140 us ~ 0.9 GB/s, far below IB
  link rate), which suggests the per-element volatile sentinel check + reset loop in the CPU reduce is a
  bottleneck at larger sizes.
